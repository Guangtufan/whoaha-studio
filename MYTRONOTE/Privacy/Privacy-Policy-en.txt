# MYTRONOME PRIVACY POLICY
Effective date: November 13, 2026

## WHO WE ARE
MYTRONOME is developed by WHOAHA STUDIO ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how MYTRONOME handles information when you use the app.

## INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We do not collect or receive personal information through MYTRONOME. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking technologies.

Settings, saved presets, custom colors, and the on-device state used to decide when to show an optional review prompt are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. We do not receive this information. You can remove saved presets in the app. Other locally stored app data can be removed by deleting MYTRONOME from your device.

## AUDIO AND SOUND FILES
Metronome sounds and interface sound effects are included in the app bundle and played locally. MYTRONOME does not request, download, or stream sound files from audio URLs or third-party sound servers. Selecting or playing a sound does not send your sound selection, settings, or listening activity to us or to an audio provider.

Some audio samples are sourced from Pixabay and used under the Pixabay Content License. Pixabay is credited in the app; its website is opened only if you choose the license link. The app does not contact Pixabay to play these bundled samples.

## HOW APPLE SERVICES ARE USED
If you choose to rate the app, MYTRONOME may call Apple's StoreKit review request. Apple controls whether a review prompt appears and handles that interaction under Apple's own terms and privacy practices. We do not receive your rating or review through this request.

When you choose to contact us, the app copies our support email address to your clipboard. You decide whether to paste it into an email. Email providers may process information you include in a message according to their own policies.

## SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES
We do not sell, share, or disclose personal information because MYTRONOME does not collect it. The app has no account, analytics, or advertising service. Apple services you choose to use, such as StoreKit or the App Store, are governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

## RETENTION AND DELETION
We do not keep a copy of your app settings or presets. They remain on your device until you remove them in the app or delete the app. We do not have user data to retrieve or delete from our servers.

## CHILDREN
MYTRONOME does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is made. The current policy is available at:
